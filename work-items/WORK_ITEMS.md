# Work Items

Active feature queue for Ivy. Game Director and Systems Designer pull from here.

## Current phase

**Phase 1 — Ivy simulator on a small brick tower.** Scope, goals, success criteria, non-goals, and
milestones M1–M4 are defined in `DESIGN.md`. Technical spec is `ivy_simulator_v0.1.md`.

Phase 1 answers two questions only: *does the simulator work*, and *does it look good*. No
gamification.

**Sequencing rule (director-level, not negotiable):** no parameter tuning, no field-precision work,
and no leaf-art polish before M1 is on screen. Get ugly ivy on the tower first.

**Stage (updated 2026-08-09):** Game Director, Systems Designer, Gameplay Architect and Gameplay
Programmer stages are complete through Stage 2, and a Gameplay Fixer pass has closed the defects it
raised. **A Code Reviewer pass is owed** on Stage 0, the M1 slice and the light field — the earlier
attempt did not complete — and QA Playtester has not yet verified anything against the current
baseline.

**Milestone reality.** M1's gate is met: recognizably ivy-shaped growth appears in
`.tmp/ui_scripts/m1_exit.png`, and the run is reproducible at 2846 segments / 1385 leaves / 82.16 m
of stem over 30 game-days. **M2's gate cannot be evaluated at all**, because AS-2 and AS-3 are
coverage numbers and no coverage measurement exists — W-021 is the critical path, and W-014 depends
on it in turn. M3 and M4 are untouched.

**Implementation order (Gameplay Architect, `IMPLEMENTATION.md` § AR-SEQ).** Work the queue in this
order, not by ID:

| Stage | Items | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| **0 — Foundations** | W-026 (`IvyParams`) → W-017 (`Conv`) → W-018 (`Hash64` / `RngStream` / `SimClock`) | Unit tests green; nothing on screen yet |

Two ordering notes that are not obvious from the IDs. W-019 lands **before** W-009: building the
light field against live raycasts and retrofitting the bake afterwards means writing the EWMA loop
twice. And the whole of Stage 0 lands before any 3D content, because `SD-CONV` is the highest-risk
detail in the project and its conformance tests are worthless once geometry already depends on a
wrong convention.
